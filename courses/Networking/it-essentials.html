Course Overview

IT Essentials is your gateway to the world of technology. This course covers the fundamentals of computer hardware, software, and networking, providing you with the essential skills needed to pursue a career in IT. Whether you're interested in becoming a help desk technician, network administrator, or IT support specialist, this course will give you the knowledge and confidence to succeed.

Course Modules

  • Computer Hardware Fundamentals
  • Operating Systems and Software
  • Networking Basics
  • IT Support and Troubleshooting
  • Career Development in IT

Course Duration:

10 weeks, twice a week, 2 hours each (40 hours total).
