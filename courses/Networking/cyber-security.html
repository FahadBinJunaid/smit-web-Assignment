Course Overview

Our Ethical Hacking & Cyber Security course is designed to equip you with the skills and knowledge needed to protect digital assets and combat cyber threats. Through hands-on labs, real-world scenarios, and expert instruction, you'll learn how to identify vulnerabilities, conduct penetration testing, and implement robust security measures.

Course Modules

  • Introduction to Ethical Hacking
  • Network Security Fundamentals
  • Penetration Testing Techniques
  • Web Application Security
  • Cryptography and Encryption
  • Incident Response and Forensics
  • Cyber Threat Intelligence
  • Ethical Hacking Tools and Methodologies

Course Duration

This course is designed to be completed in 12 weeks, with a combination of lectures, hands-on labs, and real-world projects to ensure you gain practical experience in ethical hacking and cyber security.
