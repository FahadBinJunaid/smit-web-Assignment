Course Overview

In this course, you will learn the fundamentals of 3D animation and visual effects. You will explore various techniques and tools used in the industry to create stunning animations and visual effects for films, games, and other media. The course covers topics such as modeling, texturing, rigging, animation principles, lighting, rendering, and compositing. By the end of the course, you will have a solid understanding of the 3D animation pipeline and be able to create your own animations and visual effects.

Course Modules

  • Module 1: Introduction to 3D Animation
  • Module 2: Modeling and Texturing
  • Module 3: Rigging and Animation Principles
  • Module 4: Lighting and Rendering
  • Module 5: Compositing and Visual Effects

Course Duration

The course duration is 12 weeks, with classes held twice a week for 2 hours each. The total course hours are 48 hours.
