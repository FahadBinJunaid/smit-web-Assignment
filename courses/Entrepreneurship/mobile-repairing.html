Course Overview

Our Mobile Repairing course is designed to equip you with the skills needed to diagnose and repair common issues in smartphones and tablets. From screen replacements to battery repairs, you'll learn how to fix a wide range of problems and start your own mobile repair business.

What You'll Learn

  • Understanding mobile hardware components
  • Diagnosing common mobile issues
  • Screen replacement techniques
  • Battery replacement and optimization
  • Software troubleshooting for mobile devices
  • Starting and managing a mobile repair business

Course Benefits

  • Hands-on training with real devices
  • Access to industry-standard tools and equipment
  • Expert instructors with years of experience
  • Flexible learning schedule to fit your needs
  • Opportunities for internships and job placements

Cource Duration:

10 weeks, twice a week, 2 hours each (40 hours total).
